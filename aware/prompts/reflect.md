# Reflect Prompts — Deep Reflection Sessions

Use these for deeper, more spacious reflection. Best when the user has time and is in a reflective mood.

## Prompt 1: Life Chapter
> **Trigger:** Quarterly or during transition
>
> "You're in what chapter of your life right now? Give it a title and a one-sentence summary. When you know the chapter you're in, you know what scenes to write."

## Prompt 2: Values Check
> **Trigger:** During uncertainty or decision-making
>
> "You're facing [situation/decision]. What value of yours is most relevant here? Not what's the 'right' answer — what does your deepest value want you to do?"

## Prompt 3: Growth Map
> **Trigger:** Reflective session
>
> "Think about who you were 3 years ago. What's the biggest way you've grown since then that you don't give yourself enough credit for?"

## Prompt 4: Fear / Desire Pair
> **Trigger:** When user has a big decision
>
> "For [decision], name the fear and the desire side by side. Like: 'I'm afraid [X] but I really want [Y].' Sometimes just naming both clarifies everything."

## Prompt 5: The Version of You
> **Trigger:** Deep session
>
> "What version of you are you currently becoming? Not the version you're trying to be — the version your current habits, choices, and patterns are actually creating."

## Prompt 6: Unfinished Business
> **Trigger:** When user mentions the past
>
> "What's something from your past that still has emotional weight? Not to dwell on it, but to see what it's still teaching you or asking from you."

## Prompt 7: Legacy Lens
> **Trigger:** Spacious, open session
>
> "If you lived the next 5 years exactly as you're living now — what would you have more of, less of, and wish you'd started sooner?"

## Prompt 8: The Eulogy Exercise
> **Trigger:** Rare, deep session
>
> "At your funeral, someone who knows you deeply gives a eulogy. What do you hope they say about how you lived, loved, and what mattered to you? Now — how does that compare to how you're actually living?"

## Usage Guidelines
- These are deep — use sparingly and only when the user has space
- Always check readiness: "I have a deep question if you're up for it"
- Let the user set the pace. If they go shallow, don't push.
- Capture insights in journal/insights/
- These often trigger meaningful content for /aware publish
