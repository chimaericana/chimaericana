# Morning Prompts — Start Your Day with Intention

Use these to engage the user at the start of their day. Choose one that feels right based on context and recent conversations.

## Prompt 1: Intention Setter
> **Trigger:** First interaction of the day
>
> "Good morning! Before we dive in — what's **one thing** you want to make true today? Could be something you accomplish, a feeling you want to carry, or a way you want to show up."

## Prompt 2: Energy Check
> **Trigger:** When user seems low energy or hesitant
>
> "How are you feeling this morning? On a scale from 'need more coffee' to 'ready to conquer the world' — where are you? Let me know what kind of energy you're working with and I'll match the vibe."

## Prompt 3: Focus Finder
> **Trigger:** When user has many things going on
>
> "You've got a lot on your plate today. What's the **one thing** that, if you got it done, would make everything else feel easier? Let's focus on that first."

## Prompt 4: Gratitude Opener
> **Trigger:** Random, once a week
>
> "Quick morning question: What's something you're looking forward to today? Could be big (a meeting, a project) or small (a good coffee, a conversation)."

## Prompt 5: Momentum Builder
> **Trigger:** After a good previous day
>
> "You had a solid day yesterday. What's one thing from yesterday's energy you want to carry into today?"

## Prompt 6: Fresh Start
> **Trigger:** After a tough previous day
>
> "New day, fresh start. Yesterday's in the rearview. What's one small win you can grab today to rebuild momentum?"

## Usage Guidelines
- Don't use morning prompts more than once per day
- Let the user's energy guide the depth of the conversation
- If they're busy, keep it light. If they're reflective, go deeper.
- After they respond, log to journal and offer follow-up if appropriate
