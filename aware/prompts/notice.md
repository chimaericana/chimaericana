# Notice Prompts — Become More Aware

These prompts help the user build the muscle of noticing — small observations that reveal patterns over time. This is the core of life improvement.

## Prompt 1: What Changed?
> **Trigger:** Any time
>
> "What's different today compared to yesterday, even in a small way? Something you noticed, felt, or saw differently."

## Prompt 2: Pattern Spot
> **Trigger:** After a recurring theme emerges
>
> "I've noticed [theme] has come up a few times recently. What do you make of that? Is it a signal worth paying attention to?"

## Prompt 3: Micro-Observation
> **Trigger:** Random, low-pressure
>
> "Quick notice: What's one thing about your environment right now that you hadn't consciously registered until I asked?"

## Prompt 4: People Notice
> **Trigger:** After social interactions
>
> "You mentioned [person/interaction]. What did you notice about that exchange? Sometimes the small dynamics tell us the most."

## Prompt 5: Body Check
> **Trigger:** When user seems tense or distracted
>
> "Quick body scan: Where are you holding tension right now? Shoulders? Jaw? Stomach? Just notice it — no need to fix it."

## Prompt 6: Surprise Capture
> **Trigger:** Any time
>
> "What surprised you today? It can be anything — something someone said, something you realized, or how you reacted to a situation."

## Prompt 7: Energy Shift
> **Trigger:** When context changes
>
> "Notice how your energy shifted when [event/change] happened? What was different before vs after?"

## Usage Guidelines
- "Noticing" is a skill — start with easy prompts, go deeper over time
- Record patterns in `journal/patterns/` for later analysis
- Don't over-interpret — just capture and acknowledge
- The goal is awareness, not action (yet)
