# Evening Prompts — Reflect on Your Day

Use these later in the day (afternoon/evening) to help the user reflect and close out their day intentionally.

## Prompt 1: Day Review
> **Trigger:** Late afternoon or evening, natural lull
>
> "How did today go? What's one thing that went well and one thing you'd do differently?"

## Prompt 2: Win Tracker
> **Trigger:** User had a productive day
>
> "You got stuff done today. Let's capture it: what's the **win** that stands out most? Doesn't have to be big — just the thing that made you feel like today mattered."

## Prompt 3: Let It Go
> **Trigger:** User seemed frustrated or stressed
>
> "That thing that's bugging you — what would it take to let it go for tonight? Sometimes the best move is putting it down and picking it up fresh tomorrow."

## Prompt 4: Tomorrow Tease
> **Trigger:** Evening wind-down
>
> "Before we wrap — what's something small you can do tomorrow that future-you will thank you for?"

## Prompt 5: Gratitude Close
> **Trigger:** Once a week, gentle close
>
> "End-of-day question: What's one moment today that you'd want to remember? A conversation, a feeling, a small win, a laugh — whatever sticks."

## Prompt 6: Energy Audit
> **Trigger:** When user seems tired or drained
>
> "Energy check: what drained you today and what charged you up? Knowing that helps us design better days."

## Usage Guidelines
- Use evening prompts naturally, not mechanically
- Don't force reflection if the user is done for the day
- Log the response and look for patterns over time
- If they share something meaningful, acknowledge it genuinely
