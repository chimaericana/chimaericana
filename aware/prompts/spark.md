# Spark Prompts — Creative Inspiration

Use these to spark creativity, new ideas, and different ways of thinking. Great for when there's a lull or the user wants something different.

## Prompt 1: Random Idea
> **Trigger:** User seems open or curious
>
> "Here's a spark for you: What's something you're curious about right now that has nothing to do with work? Follow that thread for a minute."

## Prompt 2: What If
> **Trigger:** Creative mode
>
> "What if you had zero constraints for a day — time, money, location, all unlimited. What would you actually do? (The answers are often more revealing than you'd think.)"

## Prompt 3: Third Thing
> **Trigger:** User is stuck on two options
>
> "You're weighing two options. What's the **third thing** you haven't considered? Sometimes the best path is the one that isn't on the table yet."

## Prompt 4: Reverse Engineer
> **Trigger:** User has a goal but no path
>
> "Imagine it's 6 months from now and you've achieved [goal]. What did you have to **stop** doing to get there? The answer tells you what's in the way."

## Prompt 5: Future Retrospective
> **Trigger:** Reflection mode
>
> "You're looking back from 5 years in the future. What decision that you made today are you most grateful for? What decision do you regret not making?"

## Prompt 6: Constraint Challenge
> **Trigger:** User feels stuck or bored
>
> "You're only allowed to spend 30 minutes on [problem] today. What's the highest-impact thing you can do in that time? Constraints breed creativity."

## Prompt 7: Inherited Belief Check
> **Trigger:** Deep reflection
>
> "What's something you believe that you never actually chose to believe? Something you absorbed from family, culture, or circumstance. Is it still serving you?"

## Usage Guidelines
- Sparks are best when the user has space to think
- Don't overuse — 1-2 per week max unless they ask for more
- After they respond, offer to capture the insight in their journal
- Some sparks might trigger a process or publishable idea
