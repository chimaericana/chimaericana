# Check-in Prompts — Goal Progress Tracking

Use these to help the user check in on their goals, projects, and commitments. Keeps things moving forward without pressure.

## Prompt 1: Goal Pulse
> **Trigger:** Weekly check-in
>
> "Weekly check-in: How are [goal(s)] feeling this week? On track, stuck, or shifted priority? No wrong answer — just a temperature check."

## Prompt 2: Next Small Step
> **Trigger:** When a goal has stalled
>
> "That [goal] you mentioned seems to have gone quiet. What's the **smallest possible step** you could take to get it moving again? Like, embarrassingly small. That's the step."

## Prompt 3: Priority Reset
> **Trigger:** When user seems overwhelmed
>
> "You've got a lot going on. Let's reset: if you could only finish **three things** this week, what would they be? Everything else can wait."

## Prompt 4: Done List
> **Trigger:** End of week
>
> "Forget the to-do list for a second. What's your **done list** this week? What did you actually accomplish, even if it wasn't on the plan?"

## Prompt 5: Goal Feel Check
> **Trigger:** Monthly deep check
>
> "Monthly check-in: Forget progress for a moment — how do your current goals **feel**? Energizing? Draining? Meaningful? Outdated? The feeling is data."

## Prompt 6: Accountability Light
> **Trigger:** When user set a commitment
>
> "You said you wanted to [commitment]. How's that going? I'm not here to pressure you — just to be the person who remembers and asks, in case that's helpful."

## Prompt 7: Energy Alignment
> **Trigger:** Quarterly review
>
> "Let's look at your commitments: rate each one from 1-10 on **energy return** (how much it gives you back) vs **energy cost** (how much it takes). The gap tells you everything."

## Usage Guidelines
- Check-ins should be lightweight, not interrogations
- Weekly is good for active goals, monthly for deeper review
- Always acknowledge progress, even if it's small
- If something keeps stalling, suggest a process or system
